EDS — Empowerment and Digital Safety — works with women and girls to provide practical training, digital safety education, awareness workshops, legal awareness, psychological support, reproductive health awareness, and computer and smartphone skills.

EDS was established on 18/08/2020 by a group of Syrian activists in Lebanon.

Five courses: Computer Skills, Legal Awareness, Psychological Support, Digital Safety, and Reproductive Health.

It covers protecting against online threats, secure browsing, phishing awareness, strong passwords, and digital privacy.

EDS is based in Barlias, Bekaa, Lebanon.
