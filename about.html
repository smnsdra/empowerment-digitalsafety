About EDS

Who we are

EDS — Empowerment and Digital Safety — works with women and girls to provide practical training, digital safety education, legal awareness, psychological support and reproductive health awareness.

The Initiative's Story

How EDS came to be

We work with women, and for women, to provide hands-on training, awareness workshops, and real support that helps you develop your skills, protect yourself, and build a stronger future in both the physical and digital worlds.

01
Chapter One

The First Step — 18 August 2020

Established by a group of Syrian activists in Lebanon.

02
Chapter Two

Listening to the Need

The initiative began by understanding the needs of women and girls in camps far from educational and social services.

03
Chapter Three

Starting Where People Were

Training began inside homes and tents, bringing practical knowledge and digital skills directly to women and girls.

04
Chapter Four

Where We Are Today

A safe space for learning and digital empowerment for women and girls under challenging conditions.

What Drives Us

Mission, vision and goals

Mission

We provide training and practical resources that help women develop their skills, strengthen their safe digital presence, and empower them to be active voices in society.

Vision

Women and girls capable of participating and driving change toward a more equitable and prosperous society.

Goals
Knowledge & Skills

Equip women and girls with knowledge and essential skills.

Opportunities & Independence

Empower them to shape their lives and create new opportunities.

Digital Awareness

Raise awareness about safe social media and internet use.

Safer Digital Spaces

Reduce gender-based online violence and digital harassment.

Our Next Chapter

From success to a bigger future

These successes have strengthened our commitment to community work and to leading meaningful change.
